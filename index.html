people ask us this stuff.

What is FlashTown?

FlashTown is our futuristic farm and private animal sanctuary in a remote part of Hawaiʻi Island. We built it so people can look closely and borrow whatever helps them.

What makes the systems regenerative?

We run on solar power and rainwater. We grow food, catch water and keep a huge pond full of life.

What grows at FlashTown?

About 30 kinds of fruit, lots of berries and more than 50 kinds of flowers. Fruit from this farm also started Fruity Puppy.

How many rescue animals live there?

More than 20 rescue cats and five rescue dogs live here without cages. We are very selective about tours because strangers can upset animals with histories and traumas they can’t always tell us about. Patreon and Fruity Puppy profits fund the sanctuary.

Is the sanctuary open to the public?

No. FlashTown is a private sanctuary. Visits are rare and selective because strangers can upset rescue animals with histories and traumas they can’t always tell us about.

Where can I follow the project?

Follow FlashTown on Patreon, YouTube and TikTok. That’s where we share what we’re building and what happens next.
